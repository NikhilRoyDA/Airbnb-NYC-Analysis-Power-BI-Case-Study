# 🗽 NYC Airbnb Data Analysis (2019) | Power BI Case Study

> “I didn’t just analyze rows and columns—I walked through New York City using data.”

Welcome to my full-scale data analysis case study using **Power BI**, where I dove into the world of Airbnb listings in **New York City** using real open data from **2019**.

This project is part of my learning journey with **CloudyML**, where I had the guidance and support of **Akash Raj** and the team. I'm proud to share this as a portfolio piece that combines **data storytelling**, **visual analytics**, and **real-world business impact**.

---

## 📌 Project Objective

To analyze over **48,000 Airbnb listings** across NYC and extract meaningful business insights that could help:

- **Travelers** make smarter accommodation decisions  
- **Airbnb** improve pricing strategy and host engagement  
- **Hosts** understand demand patterns across neighborhoods  
- **Business stakeholders** identify growth opportunities


---

## 📁 Dataset Source

- [📥 Kaggle Dataset: NYC Airbnb Open Data 2019](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/dgomonov/new-york-city-airbnb-open-data)
- ~48,895 listings  
- 16 columns including: `host_id`, `room_type`, `price`, `reviews_per_month`, `neighbourhood_group`, etc.

---

## 🧠 Problem Statements & Insights (with Business Value)

### 1. 🏙️ Which Neighbourhood Group has the Maximum Listings?
- **Insight:** Manhattan has the highest number of listings.
- **Business Application:** Indicates strong demand, tourism hotspots, and opportunity for premium pricing strategies in Manhattan.

---

### 2. 👤 Which Host Has the Maximum Number of Listings?
- **Insight:** A few hosts own/manage 200+ properties.
- **Business Application:** Airbnb can identify high-volume hosts (super-hosts) and tailor business relationships or exclusive programs.

---

### 3. 🏘️ Which Host Has the Most Listings in the Top Neighbourhood Group?
- **Insight:** The same high-volume hosts often concentrate properties in Manhattan.
- **Business Application:** Helps Airbnb optimize host support and promotional campaigns in top-performing areas.

---

### 4. 💰 What is the Average Price Across Neighborhoods?
- **Insight:** Manhattan has higher average prices; Bronx and Queens are more affordable.
- **Business Application:** Helps customers compare locations and Airbnb design location-specific price recommendations.

---

### 5. 📈 Why Are Prices Higher in Certain Neighborhoods?
- **Insight:** Proximity to landmarks (e.g. Times Square), tourist demand, and host experience contribute to higher pricing.
- **Business Application:** Airbnb can use this to build dynamic pricing models or location-based recommendations.

---

### 6. 🛏️ What is the Most Preferred Room Type in Each Neighbourhood?
- **Insight:** 'Entire home/apt' is consistently the most preferred across neighborhoods.
- **Business Application:** Hosts can prioritize full-home rentals to increase booking potential.

---

### 7. 📦 What is the Total Availability of Each Room Type?
- **Insight:** Entire homes dominate availability, followed by private rooms. Shared rooms are least listed and least booked.
- **Business Application:** Airbnb and hosts can better understand customer preferences and manage supply accordingly.

---

### 8. 🔥 Which Host is the Busiest?
- **Insight:** Busiest hosts have properties with consistently high reviews and booking activity.
- **Business Application:** These hosts may benefit from premium placement, review management tools, or automation support.

---

### 9. 🌟 Which Property Has the Maximum Number of Reviews?
- **Insight:** Certain properties near tourist hubs have 400+ reviews.
- **Business Application:** Helps identify what makes a listing "successful" — location, price, and host behavior.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

Here’s what I built using Power BI:

- 📅 **Monthly Reviews Trend** – Time-based insights to track engagement
- 🛏️ **Room Type Distribution** – Visual breakdown of shared vs. private vs. entire homes
- 📌 **Interactive Maps** – View property locations and prices across NYC
- 🎚️ **Slicers** – Filter by neighborhood, date of last review, or room type
- 🧮 **DAX Measures** – Custom calculations for averages, counts, and conditions
- 🧼 **Power Query** – Data cleaning (null handling, type conversion, filters)
